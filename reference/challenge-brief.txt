Some context
Carepatron is practice management software for health and wellness practitioners: therapists, psychologists, counsellors, physios, dietitians and more. They use it for scheduling, notes, billing, telehealth and a client portal. New practices get a 14-day free trial.

Plenty of practices sign up. Fewer get to the point where Carepatron is running their real practice, with real clients. We want to know what happens in those first two weeks that separates the practices that stay and pay from the ones that don't, and then do something about it.

The data below is invented. It's shaped like ours, but there are no real customers in it.

About 550 trial workspaces that signed up in July and August 2026, and everything they did in the product.

What we'd like
1
Read the data

2
Design one journey
Based on what you found, design one behaviour-triggered journey (email, in-app or both) that would get more practices to the moments that matter. Tell us what triggers it, who gets it, what they get and when, and how we'd know whether it worked.

3
Write one message
Write one email from that journey. Your reader is a solo therapist who checks email between sessions.

4
Ship something
Build and put live one small thing a practitioner could use: a landing page for your journey, a free tool, a calculator, anything you think would earn its place. Send us the link.

5
Tell us about the AI
Which tools you used, and one place where the AI got something wrong or made something worse, and how you noticed.
